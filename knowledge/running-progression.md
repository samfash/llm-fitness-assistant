---
id: running-progression
title: Building up running safely
source: General coaching practice; the weekly-increase rule is a heuristic with limited evidence
---
Beginners can start with run-walk intervals, for example 1 minute of easy running followed by 1 to 2 minutes of walking, repeated for 20 to 30 minutes, two to three times per week. Gradually lengthen the running portions over several weeks.

Most running should be at an easy, conversational pace, where you can speak in full sentences. This talk test is a simple way to avoid going too hard too often.

Many coaches suggest increasing weekly distance or time by no more than about 10 percent per week. This is a rule of thumb rather than a proven limit, and increases should be smaller if you feel persistent aches. Allow rest days between runs when you are new, and build volume before adding speed work.
