id: hydration
title: Hydration for training
source: General sports-science consensus summary; verify against primary sources before relying on it
---
There is no single daily fluid number that fits everyone. Needs depend on body size, climate, sweat rate and food intake. Practical guides are drinking to thirst and aiming for pale-yellow urine across the day.

For most workouts under about an hour, water is enough. Start sessions reasonably hydrated and sip during training, especially in heat. Fluid needs rise when you sweat heavily, and long or very hot sessions may call for electrolytes and some carbohydrate in the drink.

Drinking far more than you need can be harmful, because it dilutes blood sodium. Dizziness, confusion or severe headache during exercise are reasons to stop and seek advice.
