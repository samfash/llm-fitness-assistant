id: creatine
title: Creatine monohydrate
source: Paraphrased from the ISSN position stand on creatine supplementation (2017)
---
Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched supplements for improving strength, power and training capacity. A typical maintenance dose is about 3 to 5 grams per day, taken at any time of day. A loading phase is optional and not required.

In studies of healthy adults at these doses it has a good safety record. Small increases in body weight from water retention in the muscles are common early on.

People with kidney disease, or anyone taking medication or with a medical condition, should speak to a doctor first. Choose products that are independently tested for quality.
