---
id: protein-intake
title: Protein intake for exercisers
source: Paraphrased from the ISSN position stand on protein and exercise (2017)
---
For people who exercise, daily protein intakes of about 1.4 to 2.0 grams per kilogram of body weight are commonly recommended to support recovery and muscle building. Some guidance suggests the upper end of that range, and occasionally slightly higher, during a calorie deficit to help preserve muscle.

Spreading protein across the day is a practical approach. A common heuristic is about 0.25 to 0.4 grams per kilogram per meal, spread over three to five meals. Good sources include meat, fish, eggs, dairy, soy products, beans and lentils.

Total daily intake matters more than the exact timing. People with kidney disease or other medical conditions, and anyone unsure about their needs, should check with a doctor or registered dietitian before raising protein intake substantially.
