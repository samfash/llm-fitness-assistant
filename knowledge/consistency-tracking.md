id: consistency-tracking
title: Consistency and tracking progress
source: General coaching practice; not individualized advice
---
Consistency beats perfection. A reasonable plan you follow for months outperforms a perfect plan you drop after two weeks. Aim for a sustainable number of sessions per week and treat a missed workout as a one-off rather than a reason to quit.

Track a few simple metrics: number of sessions completed, the weight and reps of your main lifts, weekly average body weight and a waist measurement. Write down your top sets so that you can see when to add weight.

Progress is not linear. Plateaus are normal and usually last a few weeks. Review sleep, food intake and training volume, and consider a lighter week before making big changes.
