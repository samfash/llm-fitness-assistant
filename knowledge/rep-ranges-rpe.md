title: Rep ranges, effort and RPE
source: General sports-science consensus summary; verify against primary sources before relying on it
---
Heavier loads in the range of about 1 to 5 reps are most specific to maximal strength, while muscle growth can occur across a wide range of rep counts, roughly 5 to 30, as long as sets are taken close to failure.

Effort is often tracked with RPE, a rating of perceived exertion from 1 to 10, or with reps in reserve, RIR. An RPE of 8 means you could have done about two more reps. An RPE of 10 means no more reps were possible. Many lifters leave about one to three reps in reserve on most sets and go closer to failure on the last set of an exercise or on safer movements.

Training to complete failure on every set is not required and increases fatigue, so it is best used sparingly, especially on heavy compound lifts.
