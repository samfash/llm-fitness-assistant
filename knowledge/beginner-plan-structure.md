title: Structuring a beginner strength program
source: General coaching practice; not individualized advice
---
A simple beginner routine is three full-body sessions per week on non-consecutive days, such as Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Each session can include one lower-body squat or lunge, one hip hinge, one horizontal push, one horizontal pull, one vertical push or pull, and a core exercise such as a plank.

Use 2 to 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps per exercise with weights that leave a few reps in reserve. Focus on learning technique in the first few weeks and add weight gradually using double progression.

Add some aerobic activity on other days, such as brisk walking, to help meet general activity guidelines. Anyone with a medical condition, an injury or who is returning after a long break should consider checking with a doctor or qualified coach first.
