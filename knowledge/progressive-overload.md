title: Progressive overload and double progression
source: General sports-science consensus summary; verify against primary sources before relying on it
---
Progressive overload means gradually increasing the demand on the body so it keeps adapting. You can add weight, add repetitions, add sets, improve technique and range of motion, or shorten rest periods. Small, steady increases beat large jumps.

A simple method is double progression. Choose a rep range such as 8 to 12 for an exercise. Start with a weight you can lift for 8 reps. Each session try to add a rep or two. When you can complete the top of the range, 12 reps, on every set, add the smallest available weight increase and go back to the bottom of the range. Typical increases are around 2.5 kg or 5 lb for upper-body lifts and a little more for lower-body lifts.

If you cannot progress for several weeks, check sleep, food intake and total stress before changing the program.
