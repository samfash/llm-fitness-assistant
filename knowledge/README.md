# Knowledge base

Starter corpus of short, paraphrased summaries of widely used guidance (WHO, ISSN, AASM/SRS and general
coaching practice). It was written to demonstrate and test the retrieval pipeline.

**Before relying on it for real users:** have a qualified person review every file against the primary
sources, fix the `source` field with exact citations and URLs, and extend it. Add your own `.md` files here
with `id`, `title` and `source` front matter; they are picked up automatically (README.md is ignored).
