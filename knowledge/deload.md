---
id: deload
title: Deload weeks
source: General coaching practice; evidence for fixed schedules is limited
---
A deload is a planned lighter period of training used to reduce accumulated fatigue. Many programs include a lighter week every 4 to 8 weeks, and lifters also take one when performance stalls, fatigue builds up, sleep suffers or joints feel achy.

A common approach is to keep the same exercises but cut the number of sets by roughly 40 to 50 percent, or reduce the weights, while keeping reps comfortable. Do not simply stop all activity unless you are ill or injured.

The evidence for a fixed deload schedule is limited, so treat the timing as a practical guideline. If you feel fresh and are still progressing, you may not need one yet.
